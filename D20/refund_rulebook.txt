REFUND RULEBOOK  --  v2
========================

Three rules. ALL THREE must pass. Rules 1 and 2 decide IF, Rule 3 decides
HOW MUCH. Work through them in this order and stop at the first one that
fails -- then say plainly which one it was.


RULE 1 -- THE STATUS  (decides IF this is a refund case at all)
---------------------------------------------------------------
An order can only be refunded once it has actually arrived. Read the Status
column of the Order tab:

    Delivered       -> continue to Rule 2
    anything else   -> it has not been delivered yet. NOT covered by this
                       rulebook.

"Anything else" means anything else: Shipped, Delayed, In Transit, Cancelled,
Returned, unknown, or a blank cell. An order that has not arrived is a
DELIVERY question, not a refund question, and it is not yours to settle.

Do not treat a late order as delivered because its ETA has passed, and do not
infer arrival from anything else on the row. The Status cell is the only
thing that says an order arrived.


RULE 2 -- THE WINDOW  (decides IF a refund is owed at all)
----------------------------------------------------------
An order is inside the refund window if its Date is within 7 DAYS of today.

    within 7 days   -> continue to Rule 3
    older than that -> the window has closed. NOT covered by this rulebook.


RULE 3 -- THE PERCENTAGE  (decides HOW MUCH)
--------------------------------------------
The product category sets the refund percentage:

    CATEGORY        REFUND
    Electronics     50%
    Apparel        100%
    Furniture       30%
    Perishable       0%

A category that is not in this table is NOT covered by this rulebook.
A category at 0% is not a refusal you may issue on your own -- it is also
NOT covered, because refusing money is as consequential as paying it.


WHAT "NOT COVERED" MEANS
------------------------
It does not mean say no. It means YOU DO NOT DECIDE.

A rule that fails does not produce a refusal. It produces a HANDOFF: say
plainly which rule ran out, say that a person will follow up, and leave the
decision to them.

These are all the same forbidden thing, however reasonable they sound:

    "not eligible"          "does not qualify"
    "cannot process"        "I am unable to offer a refund"
    "the refund is denied"  "unfortunately we cannot help with this"

Every one of them is a DECISION, and a rule that ran out is precisely the case
where the decision is not yours to make. Refusing money is as consequential as
paying it.

Do not ask the customer whether they would like the case escalated, either.
Handing it to a person is not their choice and not a favour you are offering
-- it is what happens automatically the moment a rule runs out. Tell them it
is happening; do not ask permission to do it.

Anything this rulebook does not mention -- damaged goods, wrong item shipped,
partial deliveries, goodwill requests, anything at all -- is NOT covered.
Do not reason by analogy from the rules above. There are exactly three rules.


WORKED EXAMPLES
---------------
Delivered,  3 days ago, Apparel     -> all three pass, 100% -> agent decides
Delivered,  3 days ago, Electronics -> all three pass,  50% -> agent decides
Delivered,  3 days ago, Perishable  -> 0%                   -> human decides
Delivered, 40 days ago, Apparel     -> window closed        -> human decides
Delayed,    3 days ago, Apparel     -> not delivered yet    -> human decides
Shipped,    2 days ago, Electronics -> not delivered yet    -> human decides
Delivered,  3 days ago, damaged     -> no rule for damage   -> human decides
